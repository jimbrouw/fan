# Twitter/X Accounts Similar to Fan Hero
*Research: May 2026 — AI football fan art, player cards, personalised sports graphics*

---

> **Research note:** Follower counts marked [Confirmed] came from search result data. Counts marked [Unverified] are estimated from partial data or not findable via web search — verify directly on X before using. Post engagement examples are drawn from indexed web sources or page content.

## The Big Picture First

The overlap of **AI image generation + football fan personalisation + poster/card design** is surprisingly thin on X right now. Most accounts land in *one* of these three areas, not all three. That's the gap Fan Hero can own.

## Account 1: @ArtButSports — ArtButMakeItSports

**Handle:** [@ArtButSports](https://x.com/ArtButSports)
**Followers:** ~200K on X [Confirmed], ~276K Instagram [Confirmed]
**Creator:** LJ Rader (sports analyst, US-based)

### What they do
Match contemporary sports photography with classical fine art paintings. Each post pairs a real match photo with a centuries-old artwork that mirrors the composition. Not AI-generated — manual curation from thousands of saved art images.

### Content style
Single image side-by-side comparisons. Minimal text. Very clean. Relies entirely on the visual punchline. Posts during or immediately after major sports moments for maximum relevance.

### Why similar to Fan Hero
Same core idea: football + visual art = shareable moment. Audience is fans who want something more creative than a screenshot. Proven that this content category goes viral at scale.

### What they do well
- Timing: posts within minutes of viral sports moments
- Cross-sport reach (NFL, NBA, football, Olympics) builds a wide audience
- The Jason Kelce / Feast of Bacchus post got 95K+ likes and NYT coverage [Confirmed]
- NFL's official X account now tags them directly, sending huge traffic spikes

### Content gaps Fan Hero could use
- No personalisation — the fan is never *in* the image
- No football-specific focus (it's multi-sport)
- No AI generation — slow process, human dependent
- No merchandise or card output
- Football/soccer is secondary to US sports for this account

### Strong example post
NFL tagged @ArtButSports with a Jason Kelce photo. Rader replied with a 1654 painting match — 95K likes, NYT feature. [Source: Wikipedia / Awful Announcing]

## Account 2: @Footy_Headlines — Footy Headlines

**Handle:** [@Footy_Headlines](https://x.com/Footy_Headlines)
**Followers:** 265K on X [Confirmed], 172K Instagram [Confirmed]
**Focus:** Football kit leaks, boot news, AI kit design

### What they do
Primary source for kit leak intel and boot release news. Recently expanded into AI-generated concept kit tutorials and "Kit Vision" — a feature using AI models to render custom kit designs realistically.

### Content style
High-res kit mockups, side-by-side player/fan kit comparisons, official release announcements. Mix of photo drops and short-form analysis. Heavy use of visual comparison posts ("Player version vs Fan version").

### Why similar to Fan Hero
The closest account in the space to bridging football design culture with AI generation tools. They explicitly covered how to use Google's Nano Banana AI model to create concept kits. Their audience is exactly the football design enthusiast Fan Hero needs.

### What they do well
- Kit leak exclusives drive massive organic reach
- Launched "Kit Creator Showcase" — a community submission system for fan-designed kits
- Partner with kit creator brands (JerseyBird giveaways)
- High post frequency, disciplined visual format
- Kit Vision launch shows they're actively moving toward AI-generated visual content

### Content gaps Fan Hero could use
- No fan-face personalisation — kits without people wearing them
- No "put yourself in the image" moment
- Community designs exist but aren't personalised to the individual fan
- No matchday poster or player card output
- Focused on kit design specifically, not broader fan identity

### Strong example post
"Player 🆚 Fan Version" comparison posts consistently drive strong engagement. The Kit Vision launch (AI kit rendering) generated significant press coverage from Footy Headlines itself.

**Handle:** [@Art_of_Football](https://x.com/art_of_football)

**Focus:** Football-themed art prints, licensed club merchandise, apparel

### Content style
Clean, editorial-quality art posts. Announce new collections, club partnerships, and limited prints. High visual quality, brand-partner feel rather than creator/community energy.

### Why similar to Fan Hero
Same audience: fans who want football content elevated beyond a screenshot. Official club partnerships show there's a legitimate B2B route for football art brands. Demonstrates that football × design is a real commercial category.

### What they do well
- Licensed official club artwork legitimises the category
- Puma × AOF collaboration shows crossover into sportswear brand space
- Strong art direction — output looks expensive, not DIY
- 10-year track record signals brand trust

- No AI generation (manual design studio)
- No interactive or generative element for fans
- Follower count relatively modest for a 10-year-old brand
- Content is broadcast, not participatory

**Followers:** 239K Instagram [Confirmed] — X count [Unverified]
**Focus:** Vintage and retro football shirts

Sell and showcase authentic vintage football kits — Premier League, Serie A, La Liga, World Cup. Post daily drops of archive shirts. Strong nostalgia and fashion crossover audience.

### Why similar to Fan Hero

### What they do well
- Massive Instagram following for a niche product category
- Nostalgia-driven content reliably outperforms on engagement
- Global shipping positions them for international fan reach
- Community around the "kit" as cultural artefact maps directly to fan identity

- No AI, no personalisation, no "you in the kit" moment
- Product catalogue account — passive scrolling, not participatory
- No poster/card output
- No matchday or live moment tie-in
- The vintage shirt in a product photo is underutilised — could be "wear this kit in an AI poster"

## Account 5: @cardsplug — CardsPlug

**Handle:** [@CardsPlug](https://twitter.com/cardsplug) | [@cardsplug](https://www.tiktok.com/@cardsplug) on TikTok
**Followers:** 67.8K TikTok [Confirmed], 1.5M TikTok likes [Confirmed] — X count [Unverified]
**Customers:** 350,000+ [Confirmed via their own site]
**Focus:** Personalised FIFA/EA Sports FC-style football player cards

### What they do
Fans upload a photo, add their name, club, and stats, and get a real-life football card in the style of EA Sports FC 26 / FUT cards. 35+ card designs available. Physical and digital output.

### Content style
UGC-heavy: fans sharing their cards. Before/after reveals. Gift product positioning ("ultimate football gift"). TikTok-native content style.

### Why similar to Fan Hero
**Direct product overlap.** CardsPlug is the closest existing product to what Fan Hero does for player cards. Same mechanic: fan uploads face → gets personalised football output. 350K+ customers validates the market demand.

### What they do well
- 350K+ customers is massive proof of concept for the Fan Hero model
- EA Sports FC card design taps into a globally understood visual language
- Strong TikTok presence shows mobile-first, shareable output works
- Gift positioning unlocks a second revenue vector (birthdays, fan events)
- Trustpilot reviews (4000+ five-star) show strong product satisfaction

### Content gaps Fan Hero could use
- Physical card only — no matchday poster, no dynamic AI scene
- Static design — no AI scene generation (fan in a stadium, club colours, etc.)
- No mobile app — web-only flow
- No live football moment tie-in (pre-match, transfer day, Champions League night)
- No club licensing, just fan-made designs

## Account 7: @CheapPanini — No Score Draws

**Handle:** [@CheapPanini](https://twitter.com/CheapPanini)
**Followers:** [Unverified] — included in multiple "must-follow" football Twitter lists
**Focus:** Handmade football stickers, fan-drawn player portraits

### What they do
A couple manually draw their own football sticker cards — player headshots, iconic moments — in deliberately low-fi style. Self-deprecating humour ("the Picasso(s) of the modern era"). Sells prints.

### Content style
Funny, lo-fi, human. The imperfection is the point. Very shareable because of the contrast between effort and outcome. Fan community, not brand feel.

### Why similar to Fan Hero
Proves fans want to be *creators* of football visual content, not just consumers. The personalisation is in the *making*, not the output quality. Fan Hero's AI removes the skill barrier — same emotional job, much better output.

### What they do well
- Unique voice — humour + football + art in one package
- Very low production cost, high community warmth
- Sells prints, showing the commercial extension of fan art
- Relatable "any fan can do this" positioning

### Content gaps Fan Hero could use
- Human-made, slow, not scalable
- No personalisation for the buyer
- No mobile-first or app-driven workflow
- Output quality is deliberately poor — Fan Hero's output is the opposite
- Limited football club or player variety due to manual process

## The Gap: What Doesn't Exist Yet

This is the most important finding.

**There is no dominant Twitter/X account at the intersection of:**
- AI image generation
- Football fan personalisation (your face in the image)
- Matchday posters, player cards, or club-style fan graphics
- Mobile-first, shareable output

The accounts above are each strong in *one* dimension. None of them do all of it. The tools that do it (ImagineMe, PhotoGPT, bylo.ai, CardsPlug) are product sites — they don't have content creator presences that build community and drive organic reach.

**Fan Hero's Twitter/X opportunity:**
- Be the first account that is *both* the product *and* the content creator in this space
- Post examples of real fan outputs (with permission) as social proof
- Tie posts to football moments — transfer deadline day, Champions League draws, derby matchdays
- Run community competitions (submit your best AI poster, best player card)
- Lean into the "you, but legendary" content angle that drives sharing
